05 / What diagnosis can change

Sometimes, a name changes the story.

For many people, diagnosis means more than a treatment plan. It can provide language for symptoms, validation after years of doubt, and a clearer way to explain needs to other people.

A qualitative study of 506 participants found that 487 — 96.2 per cent — considered diagnosis important. Participants described validation, relief and a greater ability to communicate what they were experiencing.

The question that remains

SAFE is one research response to the challenge of recognising possible endometriosis. The psychological research points to another reason the journey matters: what it can mean to live with symptoms before there is an explanation.
